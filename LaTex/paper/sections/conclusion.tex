\section{Conclusion}

The project demonstrates the effective application of explainable AI techniques to enhance transparency and interpretability in financial decision-making. By integrating SHAP and LIME with domain-specific predictive models such as logistic regression, random forest, and neural networks, the system provides clear insights into feature-level contributions for loan default prediction, property price estimation, and stock price forecasting. The development of a user-friendly interface enables seamless interaction between analysts and predictive models, while extensive testing in realistic financial scenarios validates the robustness and practical applicability of the system. Future enhancements include integrating real-time financial data through APIs, expanding the platform to additional financial domains such as fraud detection and portfolio optimization, and incorporating advanced explainability techniques with customizable explanation levels to further improve usability and interpretability for diverse stakeholders.
